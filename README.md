# Hi 👋, I'm Vukašin Branković

## 🚀 About Me

Hi, I am Vukašin Branković, a passionate DevOps Engineer based in Niš, Serbia with 3+ years of experience in the Tech industry. I graduated from the Faculty of Electronic Engineering, University of Niš, with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science.

I specialize in designing and implementing GitOps workflows powered by ArgoCD and Kubernetes, enabling teams to achieve reliable, repeatable, and auditable deployments at scale. My core expertise spans CI/CD pipeline engineering, cloud-native security, and infrastructure cost optimization.

I am deeply proficient in a wide array of DevOps tools and methodologies, constantly seeking ways to automate processes and enhance system reliability. Dedicated to helping organizations streamline their delivery process, harden their security posture, and reduce cloud spending without sacrificing performance.
